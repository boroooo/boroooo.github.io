Hi, I’m Baorian Nuchged (traditional Mongolian script: {{< mongolian >}}<span>ᠪᠣᠷᠣᠭᠠᠨ</span><span>ᠨᠢᠴᠦᠭᠦᠳ</span>{{< /mongolian >}}, pronounced as [bɔ&#39;ɾɔːn nut͡ç&#39;gəd]).

I am a Ph.D. student in Linguistics at **The University of Texas at Austin**, advised by [Dr. Fernando Llanos](https://fllanoslucas.wixsite.com/neuroling-lab), and a member of the [Neurolinguistics Lab](https://fllanoslucas.wixsite.com/neuroling-lab).

My research lies at the intersection of **phonetics, neurolinguistics, and computational modeling**. I am broadly interested in how listeners extract information from the speech signal and how this information is represented in the brain. My current work investigates how changes in spectrally localized acoustic information shape the neural encoding of continuous speech.
